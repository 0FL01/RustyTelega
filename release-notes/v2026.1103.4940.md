### Mac users: download this version from the site once

Quill for Mac is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings. Your current copy can't install this update by itself, because it was signed the old way. Download Quill from the site once and replace the app. Your accounts and settings stay. Updates work as usual after that.

### Smoother on a Mac

- Mac windows now draw on Apple's Metal display link, which times each frame to the display.
- In Low Power Mode on a Mac or battery saver on Windows, Quill draws at most 30 frames a second. To keep full speed, turn off "Save Power on Low Battery" in Settings › Battery and Animations.
- The text cursor stops blinking after 10 seconds without typing, so an idle window draws nothing.
- Quill now uses GPUI Kit 0.9, and the built-in menus, pickers and dialogs follow your Quill language.

### Chats

- Polls have a new look, and every option lines up on the same side as the first one. Hebrew in polls wraps between words.
- Chat wallpapers the other person sets now show in your chat too.
- Clicking a notification opens the chat with the cursor in the message box.
- The story list loads all the stories, not just the first page.
- In a secret chat, the message menu offers only what secret chats allow.
- "Highlight words with emoji" is now off by default. When it's on, the first word of a draft lights up once you type a space after it.

### Settings

- Picking a search result scrolls to that setting, centers it and highlights it.

### Not yet tested on real hardware

These passed automated tests, but we haven't run them on the real thing:

- The Metal display link with real video, HDR pictures, live resizing, moving a window between displays and Low Power Mode
- The first notarized Mac build, installed from the site
