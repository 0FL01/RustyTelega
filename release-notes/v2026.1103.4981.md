### Channel direct messages

- **Writing to a channel:** the direct messages button in a channel's bottom bar now opens a real chat with a composer. When the channel charges, the field shows the price, a card explains the fee with a Buy Stars button, and the first paid send still asks you to confirm. A back arrow returns you to the channel, and the info panel opens the channel's profile.
- **For a channel's admins:** each person who writes gets a tab with their picture, name and unread badge, and All shows everything. In All, a message reads "Choose a message to reply." until you pick one, and the reply goes to that person's conversation. A tab's menu marks it read or unread, bans or unbans the person, or deletes the chat. While messages cost Stars, the chat's menu can remove or charge the fee.
- The channel's bottom bar is one even bar: direct messages on the left, Mute or Join in the middle, Discuss on the right. Before, a click on the direct messages button could also toggle Mute.

### Chats

- Chats open at the first unread message, or at the bottom when everything is read. Before, a chat could reopen high up on old messages where you last stopped.
- Clicking a chat in the list or in search puts the cursor in its composer, so you can start typing right away.

### Not yet tested on a live account

- Channel direct messages were checked with tests and offline demos only. No message was sent, nobody was banned and no fee was changed on a real account.
